Close⁠-⁠up Magic

Magic that happens in your guests' hands

Pavel moves between small groups of guests and works up close, one conversation at a time. He'll guess your phone passcode, put your watch inside a glass bottle, and your favourite film will turn up printed on his T⁠-⁠shirt. Watch the video

  • up to 60 minat the welcome drinks, while guests arrive
  • 20⁠-⁠40 minat the dinner, between courses
Why it matters

While guests are still arriving, the evening hasn’t found its rhythm yet: some are here, some are on their way, everyone has a glass in hand and the conversation hasn’t quite started. Pavel walks up to each group and lifts the mood. A trick is a strong emotion: it breaks through people’s social armour and sets them up for the celebration. It is also a true “ice⁠-⁠breaker”: guests who don’t know each other start talking about what they just saw

Stage Show

One moment the whole room shares

An interactive stand⁠-⁠up magic show for the whole room at once: comedy, strong effects, tight direction and a style of its own. Everyone gets involved. Usually runs during dinner

  • 25 minone set
  • 2 × 25 mintwo sets in one evening
Why it matters

The stage show is the core of any illusionist’s work, the thing he builds his whole life: half an hour of the most impossible things he can do. It is usually one of the highlights of the night: guests keep asking each other what just happened and remember the evening by it. And a practical bonus: if the backline has to be changed between two headliners, the 25⁠-⁠minute show holds the room while the crew works

Incognito

All evening among your guests — nobody knows who he is

Pavel acts like any other guest at your event: he mingles, he chats, and then, mid⁠-⁠conversation, the impossible happens. The most playful, prank⁠-⁠style format of the three

FAQ

Before you book

Why have a magician at an event?

Magic is a strong emotion, and it works for the whole evening. Close⁠-⁠up at the start entertains guests while everyone arrives, breaks the ice and gives strangers a reason to talk. The stage show becomes one of the highlights of the night, the moment people discuss and remember.

What formats can I book?

Close⁠-⁠up — magic right in your guests’ hands, as Pavel moves from one group to the next.
Immersive close⁠-⁠up — a separate magic zone where guests come in two to four at a time for a personal magic experience.
Parlour magic — between close⁠-⁠up and stage: guests gather around Pavel and the magic happens in front of everyone.
Stage show — an interactive show for the whole room.
After Party Magic — magic at the party, when everyone is already dancing.
Incognito — all evening among the guests, and nobody knows he is a magician.

How long is a performance?

Typically: close⁠-⁠up up to 60 minutes, though some evenings Pavel works for several hours straight; immersive close⁠-⁠up about an hour; the stage show 25 minutes; After Party Magic about 40 minutes; Incognito all evening. These are typical times, and they are adjusted to your event.

When in the evening does magic work best?

Close⁠-⁠up works best at the start, at the welcome drinks, and later between dinner courses. The stage show fits the dinner part, ideally the second or third block, when all the guests are in the room. After Party Magic belongs to the party, when everyone is dancing.

Can you make a trick specially for our company?

Yes. The magic adapts to the theme of the event: at a corporate event, a routine about your company; at a birthday, one about the birthday person’s date of birth; at a wedding, one about the couple’s love story. These routines land hardest, because guests can see the show was made for them.

What language is the show in?

English or Russian.

Does Pavel travel to other countries?

Yes, and it is no hassle at all: Pavel is constantly on the move and the logistics are well rehearsed. He performs regularly in Spain, France, Monaco, Portugal, Cyprus and Dubai, and travels to events worldwide.

How do I book?

Send the date, the city and the type of event through the form or on WhatsApp. Pavel reads every request personally and replies himself.
