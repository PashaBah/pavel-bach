Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Who is responsible

Pavel Bakhadov (Pavel Bach), Z2328844F, Bergnes de las Casas 3, 08025 Barcelona, e⁠-⁠mail pavelbah.magic@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you send a booking request: your name, a contact (WhatsApp, phone or e⁠-⁠mail), the event date or “not sure yet”, the city and country, the type of event and, if you add them, the number of guests and a comment.

Together with the request we store: the page language, the ad tags in the link you came from (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_content, utm_term), the ChatGPT ad identifier (oppref) if the link had one, the website you came from, the time, and a random request number.

Why

To reply to your request and arrange the performance — steps taken at your request before a contract (GDPR, Art. 6(1)(b)). The ad tags show which advertising brings requests (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f)). Advertising measurement in your browser runs only with your consent where the law requires it (Art. 6(1)(a)); see the cookie policy.

Where the data goes

  • Requests are stored in the request register of the site’s hosting (Cloudflare) and in a private Telegram chat.
  • Pavel gets a notification in Telegram (Telegram Messenger).
  • The site is hosted by Cloudflare.
  • Advertising measurement: Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.) and the ChatGPT Ads Measurement Pixel (OpenAI). They receive no name, phone number, e⁠-⁠mail or text from your request — only the visit, a tap on WhatsApp or Telegram, or the fact that a request was recorded, with its random number.
  • If you write on WhatsApp or Telegram, their own privacy terms apply.

How long

Requests are kept for 24 months after the last contact, unless a booking requires longer storage by law (invoices, taxes). Cookies — see the cookie policy.

Transfers outside the EU

Meta, OpenAI, Cloudflare and Telegram may process data outside the European Economic Area. Such transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, its correction or deletion, restrict or object to its use, ask for a copy, and withdraw consent at any time (“Cookie settings” at the bottom of the main page). Write to pavelbah.magic@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD, aepd.es).
