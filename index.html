FAQ

Before you book

What formats can I book?

Three: close-up magic among your guests, a stage show for the whole room, and Incognito, where Pavel spends the evening as one of the guests.

How long is a performance?

Close-up: up to 60 minutes at the welcome drinks, or 20–40 minutes at dinner, between courses. Stage show: one 25-minute set, or two sets in one evening. Incognito: all evening.

Does Pavel travel to other countries?

Yes. He performs regularly in Spain, France, Monaco, Portugal, Cyprus and Dubai, and travels to events worldwide.

What does Pavel need at the venue?

Close-up and Incognito need no stage and no setup: Pavel works among the guests. For the stage show, tell us about the venue and Pavel will confirm the details.

How do I book?

Send the date, the city and the type of event through the form or on WhatsApp. Pavel reads every request personally and replies himself.
